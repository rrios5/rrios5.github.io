Exam 1 Study Notes   Last Update: 02/22/2023 @ 2 PM


LAYOUT:


  • 50 Multiple choice questions, 2 points each
  • Questions are pulled from a pool and in random question AND answer order; therefore, no 2 tests are the same.
  • Testing Method - On-line Via Blackboard and password
  • Test Date/Available:  Check the Syllabus or email. Test are usually made available on the day following the Newtwork Game and you have until that Sunday at Midnight to take it BUT this could change, so be sure to check the emails and schedules for the actual availability times.
  • Time: 90 minutes from the time you begin and you get one try unless otherwise told via email. If you have ADA accomodations, please let me know so I can make adjustments, i.e. give you more time, etc...
  • Content Covered: Class lectures and assigned homework.

RULES:

  • This is an on-line test. YOU, the student, should be taking the test. Nobody else is allowed to help you take this test.
  • You cannot ask any questions of a live person regarding test content before, during or after the test.
  • You may NOT print,  copy or save any part of this test other than submitting it for grading. Blackboard has mechanisms that can track this while you are taking the test.
  • You may not share any answers to any questions with any other students in the class, even if they have told you they have already taken the test.
  • You may use your notes, books, power point presentations, the internet or any other static information. Again, you cannot ask for help from any other humans.
  • IF you answer an essay question, DO NOT COPY AND PASTE. Rewrite the answers in your own words. AND CITE YOUR SOURCES. Credit may not be given if your source is not cited.
  • If it is determined that any of these rules are broken, you may be dropped from the class, receive a failing grade for the class and you may also be subject to academic dishonesty policies at the University.

GRADING:

  • The test will be initially electronically graded after you press the submit button. If it gives you a raw score, bear in mind this is not be your final test grade. I will probably have to make some adjustments afterwards.
  • If you feel that there are 2 possible answers, choose the best one, but then email me your concern and reasoning behind your answer. Occasionally there are 1 or 2 questions are dropped from a test if they are determined to be not legitimate or appropriate after the fact. If I determine there are 2 correct answers for a question, or if the question is improperly structured, I will give everyone credit for it.
  • Your test grade will either be available immediately after you take the test, OR, if there is an essay or upload component, the grade will be available 72 hours on-line after you submit the test.

AFTERMATH:

  • We will discuss the test at the following class meeting, YOU SHOULD ATTEND. This is your chance to dispute any questions and humble me in front of the rest of the class.
  • If you missed the test, you may make it up ONLY with a valid medical excuse OR if I have given you permission prior to the test day.
  • If you perform poorly on the test (i.e. lower than 50%), I suggest before dropping the course, to come talk to me first. Depending on the situations, I may allow you to retake the test OR more than likely, I will give less weight to the Exam 1 grade and weigh subsequest tests higher.

TEST CONTENT:
Questions will come primarily  from the material covered in class, including items i asked you to look at on w3schools, any links or material in the announcements and any homework I assigned you including any tutorials I asked you to do.

Please note that because of the brevity of the test, I am not able to ask questions over all the topics that I felt were important for you to know, otherwise the test would be 100+ questions.

(The following data points possibly relate to at least one specific questions on the test, although not in this order. Not all of these topics will be on your test. There are multiple versions:

 

 General HTML

  • What is HTML? What can it do and NOT do?
  • How and why do you make comments in HTML
  • What are the origins and history of HTML?
  • What are the most common tags used in HTML?
  • What are the different parts of an HTML document?
  • Be familiar with all the different sorce (src) attributes.
  • How do the different spacing tags manifest on a page? i.e. what does <br> to versus <p>
  • What are <div> and <span> used for?
  • What goes and DOESN'T go in the Heading? In the Body?

CSS
  • What is CSS?
  • What is it used for? And Why do we use it?
  • How do you use CSS to change font, color, size spacing, etc...
  • How do you reference css code?
  • How do you apply css formatting to a particular part of an HTML document?
  • How do you make comments in CSS

HTML forms
  • What is a form used for?
  • How do you implement a form?
  • What are the different input types
  • What are the different modifiers/attributes you can use on different form types? i.e. maxlength, hidden...
  • How are radio buttons different from check boxes?
  • How do you limit or force certain data types, i.e. force numeric, length, in a field?

Images and Color
  • How do you manipluate color in HTML
  • How do you add images, video, audio to HTML documents
  • WHY should you use color and images?
  • How do you SCALE images?
  • Where is the best place to put color attributes?

Javascript
  • What is the HTML Document Object model?
  • What is Javascript?
  • How do you make comments in Javascript?
  • Where do you put it and refer to it in your web documents?
  • What does Javascript do and NOT do?
  • What are some of the kinds of things you can do with Javascript?
  • What are events?
  • What can trigger events?
  • How do you create and manipulate numeric AND string data in javascript?
